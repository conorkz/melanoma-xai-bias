# Journal Metrics, Cost, and Speed for a Melanoma / Dermatology AI Paper

Scope: 15 journals that publish melanoma imaging, dermatology AI, medical image analysis, or health informatics work. Data were read from publisher pages in November 2026. Where a value could not be verified, it is marked "not found" rather than estimated.

How to read the tables:
- **[P]** = publisher-reported or publisher-hosted primary data.
- **[T]** = third-party aggregator or author survey. Treat as an estimate.
- Impact Factor year is stated wherever the source states it. Elsevier and MDPI journal pages display a current "Impact Factor" without a year; because JCR 2025 shipped in June 2026, those unlabelled values are almost certainly the 2025 JIF, but this is an inference, not a publisher statement.
- CHF list prices are converted at roughly 1 CHF = 1.25 USD for planning only. Publishers set and bill the CHF price; the USD figure moves with exchange rates.

## What is the current APC for each journal, in USD or EUR, including discounts?

### Takeaway
List APCs run from about USD 2,250 (Journal of Imaging) to USD 4,570 (Medical Image Analysis). The five Elsevier and IEEE titles are hybrid: an author can publish free under the subscription route and pay nothing. Every MDPI, Frontiers, PLOS, BMC, Springer Nature, and Nature Portfolio title in this set is gold OA and cannot be published in for free.

### Cited Findings
- Medical Image Analysis charges USD 4,570 excluding taxes for the open access option; the subscription option carries no publication fee. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/medical-image-analysis/about/insights)
- IEEE JBHI is listed in the 2026 IEEE APC list as Hybrid with an OA fee of USD 2,800 and per-page overlength charges of USD 250/350. [Source](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/IEEE-Article-Processing-Charges-List.pdf)
- Computers in Biology and Medicine charges USD 3,080 excluding taxes for OA; the journal also offers a subscription route with no fee. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-in-biology-and-medicine/about/insights)
- Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine charges USD 3,320 excluding taxes for OA. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computer-methods-and-programs-in-biomedicine/about/insights)
- Artificial Intelligence in Medicine charges USD 3,440 excluding taxes for OA. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/artificial-intelligence-in-medicine/about/insights)
- Scientific Reports charges USD 2,850 / EUR 2,490 / GBP 2,290, subject to VAT. [Source](https://www.nature.com/srep/open-access)
- PLOS Digital Health's fee is USD 3,165 in 2026; the figure was checked against PLOS's own fee page on 7 November 2026. [Source](https://casrai.org/guides/plos-digital-health)
- npj Digital Medicine charges USD 4,290 / EUR 3,390 / GBP 3,090 for original research, and USD 1,735 / EUR 1,375 / GBP 1,210 for comments, brief communications, perspectives, and reviews. [Source](https://www.nature.com/npjdigitalmed/open-access)
- Diagnostics charges CHF 2,600, with a reduced CHF 550 fee for the "Interesting Images" article type. [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/diagnostics/apc)
- Cancers charges CHF 2,900. [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/cancers/apc)
- Bioengineering charges CHF 2,700. [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/bioengineering/apc)
- Journal of Imaging charges CHF 1,800. [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/jimaging/apc)
- Frontiers in Medicine and Frontiers in Oncology each charge CHF 3,150 for A-type articles (original research) and CHF 2,500 for B-type articles. [Source](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/for-authors/publishing-fees); [Source](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/oncology/for-authors/publishing-fees)
- BMC Medical Imaging charges GBP 2,490 / USD 3,290 / EUR 2,790, subject to VAT, with country-tiered pricing under a Springer Nature pilot. [Source](https://link.springer.com/journal/12880/submission-guidelines)

- Elsevier states that APCs may differ by author circumstances and that institutional agreements may fully or partly cover the charge. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/artificial-intelligence-in-medicine/about/insights)
- Springer Nature offers APC waivers for corresponding authors in the lowest-income countries and country-tiered pricing for BMC titles. [Source](https://link.springer.com/journal/12880/submission-guidelines)
- PLOS runs a Publication Fee Assistance program, but requests must be made before or during review and are not considered after acceptance. [Source](https://plos.org/publish/fees/)

### Inferences
- For a student with no APC budget, the cheapest legitimate path to a high-impact venue is a hybrid Elsevier or IEEE journal under the subscription route: MIA, CIBM, CMPB, AIM, or JBHI would cost USD 0, at the price of a paywalled article. Most of these allow green OA deposit of the accepted manuscript.
- Among gold OA titles, the cost order is Journal of Imaging (CHF 1,800) < Diagnostics (CHF 2,600) < Bioengineering (CHF 2,700) < Scientific Reports (USD 2,850) < Cancers (CHF 2,900) < Frontiers (CHF 3,150) < PLOS Digital Health (USD 3,165) < BMC Medical Imaging (USD 3,290) < npj Digital Medicine (USD 4,290) < Medical Image Analysis (USD 4,570).
- Cancers and Bioengineering cost roughly the same in CHF but Cancers carries a higher JIF and a higher CiteScore; Bioengineering has the faster median first decision in this set.

### Gaps
- No publisher in this set publishes a general hardship discount for individual students. Only country-based waivers, institutional agreements, and reviewer vouchers were found.
- MDPI and Frontiers do not publish USD list prices; USD figures above are planning conversions, not publisher quotes.

## What are the typical review times, including SciRev author reports?

### Takeaway
Publisher medians and author reports diverge sharply. MDPI titles are the fastest by a wide margin (about 17 to 22 days to first decision and 3 days from acceptance to publication). Elsevier titles range from 5 to 40 days to first decision but 163 to 303 days to acceptance. SciRev authors report the slowest experience at Artificial Intelligence in Medicine (4.9 months for the first review round).

### Cited Findings
Publisher-reported, 2026:
- Medical Image Analysis: 7 days to first decision, 88 days to a post-review decision, 253 days to acceptance, 12 days from acceptance to online. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/medical-image-analysis/about/insights)
- Computers in Biology and Medicine: 5 days to first decision, 56 days to a post-review decision, 167 days to acceptance, 11 days to online. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-in-biology-and-medicine/about/insights)
- Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine: 40 days to first decision, 98 days post-review, 163 days to acceptance, 5 days to online. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computer-methods-and-programs-in-biomedicine/about/insights)
- Artificial Intelligence in Medicine: 12 days to first decision, 147 days post-review, 303 days to acceptance, 7 days to online. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/artificial-intelligence-in-medicine/about/insights)
- npj Digital Medicine: median 10 days to first editorial decision, 144 days to acceptance. [Source](https://www.nature.com/npjdigitalmed/journal-impact)
- BMC Medical Imaging: median 22 days to first decision. [Source](https://link.springer.com/journal/12880)
- IEEE JBHI: average turnaround about 2.5 months, variable with reviewer availability. [Source](https://www.embs.org/jbhi/faqs)
- Diagnostics: 20.4 days to first decision, 2.9 days acceptance to publication (medians, H1 2026). [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/diagnostics)
- Cancers: 17.5 days to first decision, 2.7 days acceptance to publication (H1 2026). [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/cancers)
- Bioengineering: 16.9 days to first decision, 3.1 days acceptance to publication (H1 2026). [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/bioengineering)
- Journal of Imaging: 21.3 days to first decision, 3.6 days acceptance to publication (H1 2026). [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/jimaging)
- PLOS Digital Health: 52 days to first decision, 32% acceptance rate, 233 days to publication. [Source](https://www.researchgate.net/journal/PLOS-Digital-Health-2767-3170) (the numbers match PLOS's metrics page, but the page rendering was truncated in this session)

SciRev author-reported:
- Computers in Biology and Medicine: 1.1 months first review round, 1.7 months total handling for accepted manuscripts, 3 days to immediate rejection, overall handling rating 3.4/5. [Source](https://scirev.org/journal/computers-in-biology-and-medicine/)
- Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine: 1.9 months first review round, 3.4 months total handling, 15 days to immediate rejection, rating 2.0/5. [Source](https://scirev.org/journal/computer-methods-and-programs-in-biomedicine/)
- Artificial Intelligence in Medicine: 4.9 months first review round, 4.9 months total handling, 103 days to immediate rejection, rating 1.7/5. [Source](https://scirev.org/journal/artificial-intelligence-in-medicine/)
- Scientific Reports: 2.9 months first review round, 4.3 months total handling, rating 2.8/5. [Source](https://scirev.org/journal/scientific-reports/)
- Medical Image Analysis: no first-round or total-handling data; immediate rejection reported at 2 days. [Source](https://scirev.org/journal/medical-image-analysis/)
- Diagnostics: no handling data; immediate rejection reported at 1 day. [Source](https://scirev.org/journal/diagnostics/)
- npj Digital Medicine, BMC Medical Imaging, and IEEE JBHI: no SciRev data (all fields "n/a"). [Source](https://scirev.org/journal/npj-digital-medicine/); [Source](https://scirev.org/journal/bmc-medical-imaging/); [Source](https://scirev.org/journal/ieee-journal-of-biomedical-and-health-informatics/)

### Inferences
- A fast first decision does not mean a fast publication. CIBM's 5-day first decision is usually a desk decision; the 167-day acceptance median is the realistic timeline. AIM's 303-day acceptance median is the slowest in the set.
- The MDPI acceptance-to-publication interval (under 4 days) is the single biggest speed advantage in this comparison.
- SciRev samples are self-selected and sample sizes are not shown on the pages fetched, so the author-reported numbers should not be treated as representative.

### Gaps
- None of the publishers in this set publishes an official acceptance rate except CIBM (12%) and PLOS Digital Health (32%).
- Frontiers in Medicine, Frontiers in Oncology, and Scientific Reports do not publish median decision times on the pages fetched.
- SciRev has no usable handling data for MIA, npj Digital Medicine, BMC Medical Imaging, or JBHI.

## Which journals are fully open access, hybrid, or subscription-only?

### Takeaway
Five journals are hybrid (MIA, JBHI, CIBM, CMPB, AIM). Nine are fully gold OA (Scientific Reports, PLOS Digital Health, npj Digital Medicine, all four MDPI titles, both Frontiers titles, BMC Medical Imaging). None in this set is subscription-only.

### Cited Findings
- Medical Image Analysis, CIBM, CMPB, and AIM each state "Supports open access" and offer a subscription option with no publication fee. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/medical-image-analysis/about/insights); [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-in-biology-and-medicine/publish/open-access-options); [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computer-methods-and-programs-in-biomedicine/publish/open-access-options); [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/artificial-intelligence-in-medicine/publish/open-access-options)
- IEEE JBHI is listed as "Hybrid" in IEEE's 2026 APC list. [Source](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/IEEE-Article-Processing-Charges-List.pdf)
- Scientific Reports publishes all articles open access immediately on publication. [Source](https://www.nature.com/srep/open-access)
- npj Digital Medicine is a fully open access journal with CC BY licensing. [Source](https://www.nature.com/npjdigitalmed/journal-information)
- PLOS Digital Health is an open access journal; all PLOS journals are OA. [Source](https://plos.org/publish/fees/)
- Diagnostics, Cancers, Bioengineering, and Journal of Imaging all state that articles are published in full open access under CC BY. [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/diagnostics/apc); [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/cancers/apc); [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/bioengineering/apc); [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/jimaging/apc)
- Frontiers describes itself as a gold open access publisher. [Source](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/for-authors/publishing-fees)
- BMC Medical Imaging's publishing model is "Open access". [Source](https://link.springer.com/journal/12880)

### Inferences
- Choosing the subscription route at a hybrid journal removes the APC but also removes immediate public access. For a melanoma AI paper that depends on code and data sharing, the OA route is usually the better fit if the fee can be covered.
- All gold OA titles here use CC BY, which permits reuse of figures in later work, a practical advantage for a thesis or follow-up paper.

### Gaps
- None found for access model. This is the best-covered question in the set.

### Inferences
- Journal of Imaging is the only title in the set that is not SCIE. Its JIF of 3.8 comes from ESCI, which Clarivate began assigning JIFs to in 2023. A student whose institution counts only SCIE should treat it as a lower-tier option.
- CIBM's omission of SCIE on its own indexing list is almost certainly a display gap rather than a real de-indexing: the journal has a 2025 JIF of 6.3, which requires SCIE coverage. This should be verified in Clarivate's Master Journal List before submission.
- PLOS Digital Health's WoS status is genuinely ambiguous across trackers. It has a 2025 JIF of 7.7, so it is in the WoS Core Collection in some form, but whether the collection is SCIE or ESCI is unresolved.

### Gaps
- No single authoritative Clarivate Master Journal List check was possible in this session; the SCIE/ESCI assignments above rely on publisher pages and index trackers.
- Frontiers in Medicine and Frontiers in Oncology indexing was not confirmed on a Frontiers-owned page.

## Which journals have clear limits on word count, abstract length, references, figures, or tables?

### Takeaway
Only Frontiers publishes a hard, numeric manuscript cap (12,000 words, 350-word abstract, 15 figures/tables). Elsevier titles cap the abstract at 250 words but set no word or figure limit. MDPI caps the abstract at 200 words and states no maximum length. IEEE JBHI is the only title with a hard page limit and mandatory overlength charges.

### Cited Findings
- Artificial Intelligence in Medicine: abstract must not exceed 250 words; no manuscript word limit found. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/artificial-intelligence-in-medicine/publish/guide-for-authors)
- CIBM and CMPB: abstract must not exceed 250 words; no manuscript word limit found. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-in-biology-and-medicine/publish/guide-for-authors); [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computer-methods-and-programs-in-biomedicine/publish/guide-for-authors)
- Medical Image Analysis: guide for authors fetched without a stated abstract or word limit. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/medical-image-analysis/publish/guide-for-authors)
- IEEE JBHI: voluntary page charges for regular papers of 8 pages or fewer and review papers of 10 pages or fewer; pages 9-10 cost USD 250 each and pages 11+ cost USD 350 each, mandatory. [Source](https://www.embs.org/jbhi/editorial-policy)
- Frontiers Original Research: maximum 12,000 words, 350-word abstract, 15 figures/tables combined. [Source](https://www.frontiersin.org/for-authors/where-to-publish/article-types)
- Frontiers Brief Research Report: 4,000 words, 250-word abstract, 4 figures/tables; Mini Review: 3,000 words, 250-word abstract, 2 figures/tables. [Source](https://www.frontiersin.org/for-authors/where-to-publish/article-types)
- Diagnostics: unstructured abstract of no more than 200 words, 3 to 10 keywords, no restrictions on color or image size. [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/diagnostics/instructions)
- Cancers: "no restrictions on the maximum length of research manuscripts, provided that the text is concise and comprehensive". [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/cancers/instructions)
- MDPI journals use a common template with a 200-word abstract; this is explicitly stated for Diagnostics and implied for Cancers, Bioengineering, and Journal of Imaging. [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/diagnostics/instructions)
- BMC Medical Imaging: no restriction on the number of color figures; offers extra imaging data sharing. [Source](https://bmcmedimaging.biomedcentral.com/submission-guidelines/preparing-your-manuscript/research-article)

### Inferences
- A typical melanoma XAI paper (6,000 to 9,000 words, 6 to 10 figures, 50 to 80 references) fits every journal in this set without trimming. The only real constraint is the abstract: 200 words for MDPI, 250 for Elsevier, 350 for Frontiers and BMC.
- JBHI's 8-page limit is the tightest real constraint, because a methods-heavy XAI paper with ablation tables will often run past 8 pages and incur USD 500 to USD 1,050 in mandatory page charges on top of any OA fee.

### Gaps
- Reference limits were not found for any journal in this set; none appears to impose one.
- Medical Image Analysis and Scientific Reports abstract limits could not be verified from the pages fetched.
- BMC Medical Imaging's abstract limit was not verified; BMC's standard research-article abstract cap is 350 words but this was not confirmed on the page fetched.

## Cost and speed recommendation for a student with a limited budget

### Takeaway
If the student can accept a paywalled article, the best deal is a hybrid Elsevier or IEEE journal published under the subscription route at USD 0. If the work must be open access, Diagnostics offers the best balance of price (CHF 2,600), SCIE indexing, topical fit, and speed (about 20 days to first decision, 3 days to publication). Journal of Imaging is cheaper but ESCI-only. npj Digital Medicine and Medical Image Analysis are the most prestigious and the most expensive.

### Cited Findings
- CIBM has the lowest published acceptance rate in the set at 12%, alongside a USD 3,080 OA fee and a 167-day median to acceptance. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-in-biology-and-medicine/about/insights)
- Diagnostics has a JIF of 3.8, CiteScore 6.9, JCR Q1 in Medicine (General and Internal), an APC of CHF 2,600, and a 20.4-day median first decision. [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/diagnostics)
- Journal of Imaging has the lowest APC in the set at CHF 1,800 but is ESCI rather than SCIE. [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/jimaging)
- Bioengineering has a JIF of 4.4, CiteScore 7.5, an APC of CHF 2,700, and a 16.9-day median first decision. [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/bioengineering)
- Cancers has a JIF of 4.8, CiteScore 9.0, an APC of CHF 2,900, and a 17.5-day median first decision. [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/cancers)
- Scientific Reports has a JIF of 4.9 and an APC of USD 2,850, with SciRev authors reporting 2.9 months to first review and 4.3 months total handling. [Source](https://www.nature.com/scientificreports); [Source](https://scirev.org/journal/scientific-reports/)
- PLOS Digital Health has a JIF of 7.7, a 32% acceptance rate, a 52-day first decision, and a USD 3,165 fee. [Source](https://www.researchgate.net/journal/PLOS-Digital-Health-2767-3170)
- npj Digital Medicine has a JIF of 18.0 (2025) and the highest APC in the set at USD 4,290, but a fast 10-day first decision. [Source](https://casrai.org/dictionary/term/npj-digital-med-impact-factor); [Source](https://www.nature.com/npjdigitalmed/journal-impact)
- Medical Image Analysis has a JIF of 14.0, CiteScore 25.8, and the highest APC at USD 4,570, but a free subscription route. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/medical-image-analysis/about/insights)
- Frontiers in Medicine and Frontiers in Oncology charge CHF 3,150 and cap Original Research at 12,000 words. [Source](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/for-authors/publishing-fees); [Source](https://www.frontiersin.org/for-authors/where-to-publish/article-types)
- BMC Medical Imaging charges USD 3,290 and reports a 22-day median first decision. [Source](https://link.springer.com/journal/12880)

### Inferences
- Ranked by cost per unit of prestige for an OA paper: Diagnostics and Bioengineering are the value picks; Cancers is a small step up in metrics for a small step up in price; Scientific Reports is mid-priced with a strong brand but slow; PLOS Digital Health and BMC Medical Imaging are overpriced relative to their metrics; npj Digital Medicine and Medical Image Analysis are prestige purchases.
- Ranked by speed to a published, citable paper: Diagnostics and Cancers (about 3 weeks to first decision, 3 days to publication) beat every Elsevier title by months. AIM should be avoided on speed grounds alone.
- A practical two-stage strategy for a student: submit first to a hybrid title under the subscription route (MIA or CIBM) to test the top of the market at zero cost, then, if rejected, move to Diagnostics or Bioengineering where the fee is payable and the timeline is short. This avoids paying an APC for a paper that might have been accepted at a stronger venue.
- Do not choose Journal of Imaging purely on price if the institution requires SCIE; the CHF 800 saving versus Diagnostics is not worth the indexing downgrade.

### Gaps
- No source was found on whether the student's institution has an Elsevier or Springer Nature read-and-publish agreement, which would change the recommendation completely. This should be checked first, before any APC is budgeted.
- No source was found on melanoma-specific acceptance odds at any of these journals. The recommendation is based on scope fit and general metrics, not on observed acceptance of melanoma XAI papers.
